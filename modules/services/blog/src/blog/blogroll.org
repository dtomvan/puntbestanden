#+begin_quote
But what is a blogroll?

It's basically just a corner in a blog that points at other blogs, creating a "chain". https://blogroll.org/

- [[https://writingslowly.com/][Writing Slowly]], about note-making, and writing, mostly
- [[https://v5.chriskrycho.com][Sympolymathesy]], I know him from his notes on jujutsu, but highly varying topics
- [[https://xenodium.com/][Xenodium]], about Emacs, software, and notetaking
- [[https://protesilaos.com/][Protesilaos Stavrou]], about Emacs, and philosophy
- [[https://jamesg.blog/][James' Coffee blog]], about smallweb, writing, etc.
- [[https://pluralistic.net/][Pluralistic]], about enshittification by the one and only Cory Doctorow
- [[https://fasterthanli.me][Fasterthanlime]], originally about Rust, but now more broadly about computers etc.
- [[https://excipio.tech/][Excipio]], about personal digital sovereignty / privacy
- [[https://veronicaexplains.net/][Veronica Explains]], Linux, retro computing, digital minimalism
